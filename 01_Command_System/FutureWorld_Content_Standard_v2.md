# FutureWorld Intelligence Content Standard v2

## Brand Positioning

FutureWorld Intelligence is an educational and strategic foresight platform focused on:

- Artificial Intelligence
- Climate Systems
- Geopolitics
- Emerging Technologies
- Energy Systems
- Environment
- Resource Security
- Future Trends

## Tone

All content should be:

- objective
- factual
- neutral
- professional
- calm
- analytical
- accessible
- future-oriented

Avoid:

- exaggerated claims
- emotional manipulation
- political polarization
- unsupported predictions
- sensationalism
- motivational overstatement

## Standard Content Questions

Every major FutureWorld content piece should answer:

1. What is happening?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What are the main drivers?
4. What are the implications?
5. What should the audience understand next?

## Brand Language

Approved phrases:

- Data-Driven Insights
- Strategic Foresight
- Global Perspective
- Future Systems
- Intelligence Briefing
- Knowledge Repository
- Educational Intelligence
- Climate Intelligence
- AI Intelligence
- Resource Security
- Future Trends

Core phrase:

Understand Today.
Anticipate Tomorrow.
Shape The Future.

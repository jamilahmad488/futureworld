# FutureWorld Intelligence Brand and Naming Standard

**Document owner:** FutureWorld Intelligence  
**Status:** Approved institutional standard  
**Effective date:** 13 July 2026  
**Version:** 1.0

## 1. Official institutional name

The official name is **FutureWorld Intelligence**.

- The words **Future** and **World** are joined.
- **Intelligence** is written as a separate word in normal text.
- The authorized abbreviation is **FWI**.
- The official domain is **futureworldintelligence.org**.

## 2. Authorized presentation

| Context | Authorized form |
|---|---|
| Formal name | FutureWorld Intelligence |
| Abbreviation | FWI |
| Primary uppercase wordmark | FUTUREWORLD INTELLIGENCE |
| Possessive form | FutureWorld Intelligence's |
| Website/domain | futureworldintelligence.org |

The following variants must not be used as the institutional name:

- Future World Intelligence
- Futureworld Intelligence
- Future-World Intelligence
- Future_World_Intelligence
- FUTURE WORLD INTELLIGENCE

## 3. Primary logo

The canonical website raster asset is:

`/assets/logo.png`

The editable master source and retained cinematic source image are:

- `/assets/brand/fwi-logo-master.svg`
- `/assets/brand/fwi-logo-source.png`

The two wordmark lines must read exactly:

1. `FUTUREWORLD`
2. `INTELLIGENCE`

No punctuation, decorative dots, missing letters, or alternative spelling is permitted inside the wordmark.

## 4. Website implementation rule

- Use `/assets/logo.png` for shared website navigation, homepage, gateway, gallery, and course interfaces.
- Report packages that retain local logo copies must use a derivative of the same approved master.
- Alternative logo artwork must not be introduced without updating this standard.
- Accessible alternative text should use `FutureWorld Intelligence logo` or `FutureWorld Intelligence emblem`, according to context.

## 5. Brand colours

The established cinematic palette remains authorized:

- Deep space black and navy
- Cyan and electric blue
- Gold and amber
- White and cool silver

## 6. Change control

Future changes to the institutional name, wordmark spelling, logo symbol, or master asset require explicit approval and a new version of this standard.

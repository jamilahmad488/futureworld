# FutureWorld Substack Standard v2

## Purpose

Substack is the long-form article and newsletter layer.

## Role

Social Media → Discovery
Substack → Explanation
Website → Knowledge Repository

## Article Structure

1. Title
2. Subtitle
3. Opening hook
4. Context
5. Main explanation
6. Key implications
7. What to watch next
8. Link to FutureWorld website
9. Closing note

## Tone

- professional
- readable
- clear
- research-based
- neutral
- globally relevant

## Recommended Length

Short briefing: 600–900 words
Standard article: 1,000–1,500 words
Deep analysis: 1,800–2,500 words

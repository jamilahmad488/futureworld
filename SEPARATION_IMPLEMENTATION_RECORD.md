## 1. Review purpose

This record establishes the operating baseline at adoption of the separation policy. It records what was and was not visible in the public website repository and the controls adopted before any future commercial engagement.

The review does not assert that no external relationship exists outside the repository. Any such relationship is subject to the transition rule in the Policy and must be classified before further activity.

## 2. Repository audit result

The public repository was reviewed for language and functionality associated with commercial services, consulting or advisory offers, commissioned work, client delivery, sponsorship, donations, pricing, subscriptions, licensing, paid access, and commercial profiling.

References to business, finance, investment, partnerships, or advisory concepts inside educational material were treated as subject matter, not evidence of an FWI commercial offer.

## 3. Channel designation

The public website and its repository are designated as the **FWI public-interest publication channel**. Public materials must carry PI, PS, or CS classification where the relationship could otherwise be unclear.

The completed channel classification is preserved as [`PI-2026-001`](governance/classifications/PI-2026-001-public-website.yml). It classifies the channel only; every formal publication still requires its own evidence, validation, disclosure, and approval record.

No confidential commercial contract, client data, unpublished advice, credentials, payment information, or restricted work product may be stored in the public website repository.

## 4. Controls activated

- Three mandatory classifications: PI, PS, and CS.
- Classification before work, funding, access, or public representation.
- Separate project identifiers and attributable financial records.
- Separate restricted storage and access for commercial records.
- Editorial control retained for PI and PS work.
- Prominent relationship labels for supported or commercial public outputs.
- Reclassification review after material scope, funder, client, access, use, or release changes.
- Independent check when the payment negotiator would otherwise control classification, editorial review, and release.
- Correction, suspension, and withdrawal authority protected from clients and sponsors.

## 5. Current limitations

This implementation separates institutional workstreams; it does not complete report-level evidence validation, missing template completion, readiness scoring, hard-block automation, or the two required end-to-end report trials. Those remain subsequent institutional-readiness steps.

The audit did not inspect private contracts, accounts, communications, or systems that were not supplied. If any exist, the Policy requires their classification before they are renewed, promoted, published, or used further.

## 6. Implementation decision

**Decision: OPERATIONAL BASELINE ADOPTED.** The public-interest channel is defined, future commercial work is subject to mandatory separation, and no conflicting public commercial offer was identified at the effective date.

Planetary Perspective

Climate systems do not stop at political borders

The global layer provides the comparative and transboundary context needed to interpret country, regional and community evidence responsibly.

Forests and Land

Water and Cryosphere

Drought, water stress, surface water, snow, glaciers and mountain systems affecting downstream societies.

Hazards and Ecosystems

Interpretation Discipline

Visible differences may reflect season, cloud, sensor, atmosphere or viewing conditions and require analytical validation.

Every public layer must identify the boundary source, data producer, observation period, spatial resolution, licence, processing method and uncertainty.

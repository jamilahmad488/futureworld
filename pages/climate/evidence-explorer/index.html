Live API connection

Begin with a country or territory, then select a state, province or region and a district, county or municipality where available. The chosen boundary is highlighted and used as the exact Planetary Computer search geometry. Large countries must be narrowed to a smaller administrative area before high-resolution evidence is loaded. Water Stress and Drought currently provides NDMI and NDVI pilot indicators; the remaining themes retain transparent evidence-status labels.

Data and Method

Evidence and boundaries must remain transparent

Administrative Geography

The viewer loads geoBoundaries gbOpen country, ADM1 and ADM2 geometries on demand and records source, represented year and licence information.

Earth Observation

The selected polygon is sent through the Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC search and Data API or item TileJSON services.

Evidence Validation

Rainfall, climatic water balance, hydrology, official records and field evidence must complement satellite indicators before public conclusions are confirmed.

Boundary Neutrality

Displayed administrative boundaries support geographic analysis and do not express an FWI position on sovereignty, legal status or disputed territories.

Operational status: worldwide country and subnational boundary discovery, polygon-based searches, NDMI and NDVI visualization, dynamic legends, evidence summaries and optional point sampling are included for deployed-browser validation. The current implementation uses geoBoundaries ISO alpha-3 catalog entries for direct compatibility with its boundary API; UN M49 harmonization remains a formal data-governance enhancement. Each analytical product must retain its producer, licence, date, resolution, processing method and limitations.

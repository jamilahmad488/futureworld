Provincial Evidence Interface

Map integration will begin with focused, verifiable layers

Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Climate Evidence Map — Planned PilotThe first analytical pilot is intended to prioritize forest and vegetation condition, watershed terrain, fire evidence, climate stress and restoration monitoring for selected areas, beginning with data-rich and field-verifiable landscapes.

Administrative boundaries, intervention sites and community information must be verified before public display. Sensitive or personally identifiable field information will not be published.

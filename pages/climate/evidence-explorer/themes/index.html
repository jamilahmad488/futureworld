Water Stress and Drought

Inspect moisture and vegetation response through operational Sentinel-2 NDMI and NDVI layers, while separating these satellite signals from a complete drought or community water-access assessment.

  • NDMI moisture-condition colour layer and legend
  • NDVI vegetation-condition supporting layer
  • Natural-colour satellite context
  • Rainfall, water balance, hydrology and field evidence still required

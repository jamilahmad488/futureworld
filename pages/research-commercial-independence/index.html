Research & Commercial Independence

FutureWorld Intelligence classifies every workstream before work begins so public-interest research cannot be confused with—or controlled by—commercial services.

Three Separate Lanes

Classification follows substance, not the name of a payment

PI

Public-interest research

Independently initiated for broad public benefit.

  • FWI controls the question, methods, findings, and release.
  • Public access is the default.
  • No external party purchases or controls conclusions.
PS

Partner-supported public-interest research

Public research supported by a named external party.

  • Support and the partner's role are disclosed.
  • FWI retains editorial and correction authority.
  • Public release remains a project objective.
CS

Commercial services

Paid, commissioned, licensed, customized, or client-directed work.

  • Separate contract, project code, records, and access.
  • May be confidential or privately delivered.
  • Any public version is unmistakably labelled commercial.

Editorial Firewall

Payment never buys a finding

Non-negotiable: no funder, sponsor, donor, partner, or client may purchase a conclusion, suppress a material finding, bypass a correction, weaken validation, or present commercial work as independently initiated public-interest research.
Public-interest control

Editorial authority controls methods, evidence, conclusions, validation, release, correction, and withdrawal.

Permitted client control

A commercial client may define a lawful brief, users, delivery format, schedule, confidentiality, and acceptance criteria.

Independent review

For supported or public commercial work, the payment negotiator cannot be the only classifier, reviewer, and approver.

No hybrid category

Mixed work must be divided into separately governed work packages rather than blurred under one label.

Operational Separation

Different records, access, finance, and disclosures

Financial

  • Distinct PI, PS, and CS project identifiers
  • Attributable income, costs, support, and staff time
  • Commercial invoices never represented as donations

Information

  • Separate project roots and access groups
  • No client records or confidential data in the public repository
  • No unauthorized reuse for research, training, or another client

Public disclosure

  • Classification shown near the title
  • Support, payment, client role, and conflicts disclosed
  • Labels preserved in promotion and downloadable files

Current Baseline

The website is the public-interest publication channel

A repository review dated 13 July 2026 found no public commercial-services offer, pricing or payment flow, client portal, donation mechanism, commissioned-work catalogue, or sponsored publication. Future commercial activity must be classified and separated before work, funding, access, promotion, or publication begins.

This designation does not retroactively validate every report. Report-level evidence validation, scoring, automated hard blocks, and end-to-end trials remain separate readiness controls.
